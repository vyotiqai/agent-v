# Reference images

Screens by other designers that the owner collected for inspiration. They are **not ours**. They
are kept here only so design decisions can point to them. Nothing in them is copied into Agent V:
not layouts, colours, illustrations or wording. If this repository is ever made public, remove
this folder first.

The rule for using them (stage 2 review, 2026-09-25): take the idea behind a reference, then
express it in Agent V's own Ledger language (paper and ink, serif content, signatures). If a
drawing could be mistaken for the reference, it has gone too far.

## What they have in common

The owner's feedback was that the Ledger screens were **too text-heavy**. Every one of these
references reads in about a second, and they get there the same few ways:

1. **Numbers as anchors.** One large number says more than a sentence (94%, 3 meetings).
2. **Blocks with rhythm.** Content sits in a few blocks of different sizes, not in one long
   column of equal rows.
3. **Icons do the labelling.** A small mark tells you what kind of thing an item is, so the
   words can be shorter.
4. **Round icon buttons** for secondary actions; one clear primary button.
5. **Voice is first class.** Listening has its own screen with your words and a live level.
6. **Atmosphere in the dark.** A soft light makes a dark screen feel warm, not empty.
7. **Few words, lots of space.**

## How Agent V uses these ideas

**Update, 2026-09-25:** the table below was a first attempt that kept Ledger's paper and serif.
It was rejected as not clean or structured enough, and Ledger was dropped. The new direction
(D34 in [02-app-map.md](../02-app-map.md), canvas page "New direction (proposal)") uses the
references more directly: a dark top with your day in one sentence, a light area of large
rounded blocks, one modern typeface, and dark, full-screen voice with a soft blue light. It stays
original in its content, wording and structure; it does not reproduce any single reference's
layout.

| Idea | In the first attempt (canvas page "Rebalanced Ledger (rejected)") |
|---|---|
| Numbers as anchors | Today opens with a tally, three large figures: waiting on you, on my desk, filed overnight. A job shows its step as 3/5 |
| Blocks with rhythm | Flat paper-tone blocks with no shadows. At most one **coloured** block per screen: on Today it is the blue "Waiting on you" block, because blue only ever means that |
| Icons do the labelling | Every item has a mark: email, question, web, watch, file. Signing items end with a pen mark |
| Round icon buttons | Edit, Later and Don't send on a signature; attach and speak in the hand-off line |
| Voice is first class | "Speaking to Agent V": your words in large serif, older words fading, a voice level drawn as fine ink rules (the Ledger's own lines) |
| Atmosphere in the dark | Night appearance is warm ink with a soft **desk-lamp** light at the top, not a coloured glow. The lamp is light, not a signal, so it never appears on controls |
| Few words | One line per item. A second line appears only when it carries risk ("Can't be undone") |

## What we deliberately leave behind

(Written for the first attempt. The owner has since chosen a dark top with a light content area
and a blue glow, so those two are now in; the rest still holds.)

- Pastel colour tiles. Colour in Agent V always means something (decision D27).
- Blue or purple glow backgrounds, glass bars, grain and blur effects.
- Emoji, photos and avatars inside greetings.
- A dark top with a white sheet sliding over it. The first draft did this and was rejected.
- Floating pill toolbars and tab bars (decision D24).
- Any specific layout, proportion or wording from these images.
